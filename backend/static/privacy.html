2. Personal & Telemetry Data We Process

Account Identity

Full name, email address, and optional phone number verified via 6-digit cryptographic OTP. Used for passenger profiles and conductor dispatch authorization.

Live GPS & Transit Telemetry

Passenger boarding/alighting stop IDs, approximate GPS coordinates when searching routes, and real-time vehicle AIS-140 beacon coordinates.

Emergency Safety Data (112 / SOS)

Emergency contacts, ambient audio snapshot (buffered for 5s prior to police PCR transmission), and live bus coordinate packets transmitted to Delhi Police (112) and DTC Command Room.

Conductor Ticketing & Cash Ledger

Ticket serial numbers, fare amounts, payment modes (UPI / Cash), and duty timestamps retained for statutory audit compliance.

3. Data Retention & Automatic Purge Schedule

Live GPS Telemetry
Rolling 90-day retention, then automatically anonymized.
Emergency SOS Logs
180 days retained for Delhi Police inquiry & DTC safety auditing.
Ticketing & Fare Records
3 years statutory retention required under transit auditing regulations.
User Account & Profile
Purged immediately upon triggering 'Delete Account' in Account settings.

4. Rights of the Data Principal (Your Rights)

Right to Access & Portability

You can download a complete structured JSON archive of your personal data, rides, and saved places anytime from your Account page.

You have full control to edit your profile, saved commute hubs, preferred language, and emergency contacts instantly.

Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten)

Tapping 'Delete Account' cascades an immediate deletion across profiles, sessions, emergency contacts, ride history, and tokens with zero retention.
